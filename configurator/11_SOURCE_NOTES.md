## Inventory warning

These are manufacturer/family references, not the mart's actual stock. Before enabling customer requests, populate the catalog with the business's current SKUs, actual selling prices, exact dimensions, warranty information, local availability and current supplier data.

## Why current hardware is not hardcoded into UX

PC hardware changes frequently. The UI/logic must operate on product IDs and attributes. Product data should be replaceable without changing the decision tree or components.
