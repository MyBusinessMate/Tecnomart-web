5. Cards vs. Cardless Editorial Surfaces

Strict compliance with /cards-component-patterns-auditor: cards reserved strictly for interactive items.

✓ Card Permitted

Interactive Product & Input Modules

Cards with subtle borders and shadows are strictly reserved for clickable products, selectable specs, form inputs, and checkout price summaries.

Allowed Card Locations
Popular Picks, Cart Summary, Trade-In Form, Contact Form
Keeps user focus strictly centered on direct actionable paths.
✗ Cards Removed (Blended Directly on Canvas)

Editorial & Informational Content

Eliminated nested boxed cards from informational and legal sections. Content now sits seamlessly on the canvas (#f7f8fa) with clear typographic hierarchy.

Refactored to Cardless
Student Perks, Accepted Colleges, Compare Empty State, Terms, Privacy, About
Eliminates nested box fatigue and improves reading rhythm.
